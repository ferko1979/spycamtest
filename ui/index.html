This app runs in the background (tray) and powers the web scanner. Network scan results are returned to the website; LAN network details are now included in the scan payload.
Activity tracking (work verifier)
When ON, this records the active application, its window title, and time spent — so a work-activity report can be produced. It does not capture keystrokes, screen contents, clipboard, or passwords. While it runs, the tray shows “Activity tracking ON”. You can turn it off here or from the tray at any time, and view exactly what is recorded.
Network & camera scan
Passive discovery reads your device's ARP/neighbor table. Active discovery (optional) sends TCP connection probes across your own subnet to find devices — including likely cameras — that are not yet in the table.
